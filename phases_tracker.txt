The reward's payout amount/currency always come from the campaign's snapshotted rule, never the webhook's revenueAmount - so a mismatch can never corrupt payout math - but the reward is held PENDING (hold_reason=CURRENCY_MISMATCH) instead of auto-promoting to ELIGIBLE, requiring a manual admin decision (approve path unavailable while PENDING; reject is).
